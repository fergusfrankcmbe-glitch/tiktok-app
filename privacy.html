Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy covers the Video Draft Uploader ("the application"), a personal tool operated by YOUR NAME. The application is used by its operator on their own TikTok account only and has no other users.

Information collected

The application requests the following TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload — to send video files to the connected account's TikTok drafts inbox.

When the operator authorises the application, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token. These are stored only in an environment variable on the operator's own computer. The application collects no information about any other person.

How information is used

Tokens are used solely to call TikTok's Content Posting API in order to place a video in the connected account's drafts inbox. Video files are transmitted directly from the operator's computer to TikTok's servers.

Sharing

No information is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. The only party receiving data is TikTok itself, as necessary to perform the upload.

Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the operator's computer until deleted. Access may be revoked at any time from TikTok's account settings, under connected apps, which immediately invalidates them.

Analytics and tracking

The application uses no analytics, cookies, or tracking of any kind. This website is a static page and sets no cookies.
